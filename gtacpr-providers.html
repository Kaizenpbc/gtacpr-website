Provider Requirements

We maintain high standards to ensure every GTACPR-certified student receives exceptional training.

Qualifications

  • Current first aid / CPR instructor certification
  • Minimum 2 years teaching experience with an approved provider (Red Cross, St. John Ambulance, FAST, or equivalent)
  • Valid and current CPR and First Aid credentials
  • Clean Vulnerable Sector Check (within 12 months)

Operational Requirements

  • Commercial general liability insurance (minimum $2M)
  • Suitable training facility with adequate space and ventilation
  • Professional-grade training equipment (mannequins, AED trainers)
  • Commitment to GTACPR curriculum standards and quality assurance
  • Participation in annual provider review and skills verification
